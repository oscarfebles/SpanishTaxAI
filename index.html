How does this compare to a €1,200 DNV lawyer?

I'm a US 1099 contractor — do you handle this?
Yes — US documentation is our specialty. The Spanish system doesn't recognize 1099 as a registered self-employment status the way Spain treats alta autónomo. We provide CPA letter templates, alternative proof-of-self-employment workflows, and the exact document combination UGE-CE accepts. Same for W-2 employees (Certificate of Coverage workflow under the US-Spain treaty) and S-Corp owners (structure optimization for the application).
What if my DNV gets rejected?
Outright rejection is rare. Far more common is a "subsanación" — a UGE-CE request for additional documents within 10 days, typically arriving on day 20 of the procedure. Our system includes pre-written subsanación response templates for the most common requests (bank statements not stamped, self-employment proof, income gap with savings), plus AI-assisted analysis of your specific case. The DNV Pro Review tier includes asesor review of your subsanación response before submission.
Is the €88.64 Tarifa Plana automatic?
No. The Tarifa Plana (€88.64/month for the first 12 months instead of ~€299.56) must be explicitly requested when you register with RETA. It is not applied by default. It also requires that your RETA registration is filed on or before the day your activity begins — late filing under RD 84/1996 art. 35.1 can disqualify you from Tarifa Plana even if you tick the box, because the registration may be treated as forced rather than voluntary. This is the single most common preventable mistake: €2,500 quietly lost over the first 12 months.
