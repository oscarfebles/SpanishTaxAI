CPA Letter — Single-member LLC · For US LLC owners applying for Spanish DNV
How to use: This template is for owners of a US single-member LLC (SMLLC) that is treated as a "disregarded entity" for federal tax purposes (income flows directly to your personal Schedule C). If your LLC has elected to be taxed as S-Corp, use the S-Corp template instead.
⚠ Critical: LLC + Spain = mandatory CFC analysis. A foreign LLC where you're the sole member triggers Spanish transparencia fiscal internacional rules (Art. 91 LIRPF). Spain may impute passive or low-taxed LLC income to you regardless of distributions. This is one of the most complex tax situations for DNV applicants. Do not file Spanish taxes without specialist review.
