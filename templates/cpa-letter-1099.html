CPA Letter — 1099 Sole Proprietor · For US contractors applying for Spanish DNV
How to use this template:
  1. This letter must be drafted and signed by YOUR CPA (Certified Public Accountant) — not by you.
  2. Edit the yellow fields with the relevant information (firm address, your details, dates).
  3. Send the completed template + email request (see Email to CPA) to your CPA.
  4. Once your CPA returns the signed and stamped version on their firm letterhead, you must:
    • Apostille via US Department of State (federal) or relevant state Secretary of State
    • Get it sworn-translated into Spanish by a traductor jurado
  5. Submit with your DNV application to UGE-CE.

⚠ Critical reminders for your CPA:
  • Letter MUST be on firm letterhead — do not just print this HTML as final document. Your CPA should retype on their official template, retaining the structure and content.
  • CPA's LICENSE NUMBER must be visible — UGE-CE specifically checks this.
  • Letter must be SIGNED in wet ink (digital signatures are sometimes rejected for apostille).
  • Firm STAMP/SEAL must be applied if the firm uses one (common in larger firms).
  • Letter must be DATED within 90 days of your DNV application submission.
  • After signing, apostille via US Department of State or Secretary of State (depends on document type and state).
  • Then sworn-translate into Spanish.
